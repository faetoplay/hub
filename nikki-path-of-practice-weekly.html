Week 1

Nov 2 – 8 · Setup and Stage I

Build everything, finish both Stage I lists, and get the training loop running. This is the heaviest week of hands-on play; after this it's mostly daily collecting.

Setup Days 1–2

  • How to
    1. Quests menu → track the Itzaland main story → play to the Chapter 1 complete screen.
  • How to
    1. World quest list → follow markers.
    2. Heart of Infinity → claim the free Visionary Dreamer sketch → craft and equip it.
  • How to
    1. Wearing Visionary Dreamer, use Itzaland Blueprint: Construction at each site.
    2. Enter each once so production starts.
  • How to
    1. Follow the quest markers. Finishing it unlocks Build on the Land II.
    2. Heart of Infinity → Construction: Building Exterior → unlock Snail Beauty Salon → build it at its site.
  • How to
    1. Spira Stylist's Guild → Hilsetti. Then Snail Ranch → Mollipopo. Then Construction Area → both Shroomlings.
    2. Heart of Infinity → Construction: Building Exterior → unlock Path of Practice → build it.
    3. Beat Mollipopo's styling challenge, invite her; invite Marimimi.
    4. Talk to Marimimi at the Salon. Craft a Gilded Book Stack (1 Brush Beaver Fur) and place it inside. Run Marimimi through one Discovery training event. Place a second book stack. Talk to both to finish.

Daily loop Set it up once

  • How to
    1. Path of Practice → Dispatch → Drink Shop → Marimimi.
    2. Path of Practice → Training → Mollipopo → a Daily event.
  • How to
    1. Craft Forest Message Bookshelf ×1, Gilded Book Stack ×3, Simple Low Ceramic Flowerpot ×1. Place all inside.
    2. Scheme Book → activate. +10% EXP on Daily events.

Build on the Land I Stage I · A Handful of Shroomlings

  • How to
    1. Cheapest: Plain Wood Stool (1 Thornfruit), Plain Wood Tea Table (1 Tailor Beetle). You'll place them anyway.
  • How to
    1. Inside the shop, construction mode, place furniture until the meter reads Lv. 3.
  • How to
    1. 200 → 400 → 700 → 1,100 Shroomberry.
    2. Lv. 5 needs Fruity Slow Drink Set active: Ripple Ring Vase ×1, Plain Wood Tea Table ×1, Plain Wood Stool ×2.
  • How to
    1. 300 → 500 → 1,000 → 2,500 Shroomberry + 3,000 Stone.
    2. Lv. 5 needs Warm Bath Set active: Painted Round Stand ×1, Plain Wood Stool ×1, Simple Low Ceramic Flowerpot ×1.
  • How to
    1. Drink Shop Lv. 5 letter, Bath Lv. 5 letter, Mollipopo's Bath conversations. Claim each in the letters tab.

Build on the Land II Stage I · Bustling with Shroomlings

  • How to
    1. About 3 hours of Salon production at Lv. 1. Collect before the 5,000 cap.
  • How to
    1. Mollipopo + Marimimi + your first recruit (see Companions below).
  • How to
    1. Talk to your recruited companions inside the building each visit until a letter prompt appears.
  • How to
    1. Lv. 2 hands you free furniture. Place all of it inside.
  • How to
    1. Already done if you followed the Daily loop step above.

Companions First recruit

  • How to
    1. Path of Practice → Recommended list. Aricheche is inside the Shroomling Activity Center. Talk, win the styling challenge, invite.
    2. If no challenge option appears, the companion hasn't been recommended yet or you have no free slot.

Practical Challenges

Nothing yet. Path of Practice Lv. 3 (above) clears the first of the two gates.

Week 2

Nov 9 – 15 · Stage II

Push the Drink Shop and Bath to Lv. 10. They fund every other upgrade, so this week is about production first and event tasks second.

Setup

Done. Nothing new this week.

Daily loop

  • How to
    1. Lv. 4 = 8,000 Mud. Lv. 5 = 15,000 Mud + 10,000 Stone with Deep Learning Set active.
    2. Lv. 5 gives you the Radiant Inspiration furniture already crafted. Place it and activate it. You now have 2 scheme slots.
  • How to
    1. Aricheche or Shroomthoven → Salon. Marimimi → Bath. Any Drink Shop-capable companion → Drink Shop.

Build on the Land I Stage II · Shroomling Crossing

  • How to
    1. Lv. 10 needs Playful Warm Bath Set active + 8,000 Shroomberry + 20,000 Stone.
    2. Place every piece of five sets (Refreshing, Tranquil Fragrant, Blissful, Warm, Heartfelt Fragrant) and activate all five in the Scheme Book.
  • How to
    1. The scheme furniture counts. Top up with Colorful Parasol (50) or Plain Wood Shelf (50).
  • How to
    1. Lv. 10 needs Honey Drink Dining Set active + 3,600 Shroomberry + 10,000 Stone. Lv. 15 = 7,500 Shroomberry + 30,000 Stone.
  • How to
    1. Co-op → invite a friend → they press Like at one of your buildings. r/InfinityNikki co-op threads work if nobody's online.

Build on the Land II Stage II · A Stream of Shroomlings

  • How to
    1. Lv. 5 = 3,000 Shroomberry + 3,000 Stone (Silky Shell Beauty Set active). Lv. 10 = 9,000 Shroomberry + 50,000 Stone (Pleasant Dining Set active).
  • How to
    1. Salon Lv. 5 letter, plus Marimimi's (or Nakaka's / Urili's / Fussi's) Salon conversations.
  • How to
    1. The three remaining no-quest companions below get you there.
  • How to
    1. Place the free furniture from Salon Lv. 4, 5, 8, 10. Path of Practice Lv. 5 from the Daily loop step above likely covers its part.

Companions The three no-quest recruits

  • How to
    1. Adventurer camp south of Giant Vine Forest. Wait for recommendation → talk → challenge → invite.
  • How to
    1. Near the Great Exploration Camp. Same routine.
  • How to
    1. Woods outside Shroomville. Same routine. Put him on the Salon.

Practical Challenges Second gate

  • How to
    1. Itzaland styling challenge map → each group under the Sovereign of Sexy → Perfect rating, not just a win. Level and glow up your Sexy pieces if needed.
    2. Once done (with Path of Practice at Lv. 3+), the Practical Challenge tab appears.

Nov 23 – 26 · ends 2:49 PM Eastern

Build on the Land II

Companions

Practical Challenges

After the 26th: Path of Practice, the companions, and Practical Challenges all stay. Keep leveling at your own pace for the Lv. 15 headwear (Dappled Capture) and Lv. 25 shoes (Midnight Walk).

Built for a fresh start. The goal is every pull currency that expires on the 26th, plus the permanent Diamond letters and Resonite Crystals that fall out along the way. Stage I and II of both events are done by about day 12; the rest of the month is buffer. Nothing here needs Lv. 30 buildings.

Bath first

Hot Spring Stone is the bottleneck for every building. Each Bath level is a permanent raise to the slowest resource.

Collect every 3–4 hours

Until Lv. 5, caps are 2,000 Shroomberry and 5,000 Mud. Anything over the cap is lost.

Train while you sleep

Companion EXP only accrues during a running session. Start one every time you log off.

Schemes immediately

Activate every production scheme the moment you get it. Warm Bath Set alone is +75% public bath gain.

Day 1

One long session · all four buildings

Everything below is quests and Bling. Do it in one sitting so production starts tonight.

  • How to
    1. Hilsetti at the Spira Guild → Snail Ranch → Construction Area → Heart of Infinity node → build → Mollipopo challenge → invite both → Marimimi at the Salon → craft/place book stack → one Discovery training with Marimimi → second book stack → talk to both.
  • How to
    1. Plain Wood Stool (1 Thornfruit) or Plain Wood Tea Table (1 Tailor Beetle). Ten of them covers the craft count and most of the decoration bar.

Days 2 – 5

Bath rush · both Stage I lists

All Shroomberry goes to the Bath until it hits Lv. 5, then alternate. Mud goes straight into Path of Practice.

  • How to
    1. 300 → 500 → 1,000 → 2,500 Shroomberry + 3,000 Stone. Warm Bath Set: Painted Round Stand, Plain Wood Stool, Simple Low Ceramic Flowerpot.
    2. Lv. 5 raises Stone to 10/min, the cap to 30,000, and unlocks the “Some Insight After the Bath” letter.
  • How to
    1. 200 → 400 → 700 → 1,100 Shroomberry. Set: Ripple Ring Vase, Plain Wood Tea Table, Plain Wood Stool ×2. Cap jumps to 30,000 so you can stop collecting every 3 hours.
  • How to
    1. 500 Mud, then 3,000 Mud. Deep Learning Set: Forest Message Bookshelf, Gilded Book Stack ×3, Simple Low Ceramic Flowerpot.
  • How to
    1. Letters: Drink Shop Lv. 5, Bath Lv. 5, Mollipopo's Bath conversations. Path of Practice: talk to Aricheche inside each visit.

Days 6 – 12

Everything to Lv. 10 · both Stage II lists

Bath to 10 first, then Drink Shop, then Salon, then Path of Practice to 5. Each Lv. 10 is a Resonite Crystal on its own.

  • How to
    1. Lv. 10 needs Playful Warm Bath Set active, 8,000 Shroomberry, 20,000 Stone. Stone goes to 20/min.
    2. Five sets: Refreshing, Tranquil Fragrant, Blissful, Warm, Heartfelt Fragrant. Place every piece, activate all five.
  • How to
    1. Lv. 10: Honey Drink Dining Set active, 3,600 Shroomberry, 10,000 Stone. Letter “I'll Make Drinks That Satisfy Everyone”.
    2. Lv. 15: 7,500 Shroomberry, 30,000 Stone. Check the event page for the exact Stage II target.
  • How to
    1. Lv. 5: 3,000 Shroomberry + 3,000 Stone (Silky Shell Beauty Set). Letter “Snails Are the Shroomlings' Best Friends”.
    2. Lv. 10: 9,000 Shroomberry + 50,000 Stone (Pleasant Dining Set).
  • How to
    1. Lv. 4 = 8,000 Mud. Lv. 5 = 15,000 Mud + 10,000 Stone, Deep Learning Set active. Letter “Impress All Shroomlings With My Design”.
  • How to
    1. Camp south of Giant Vine Forest; near the Great Exploration Camp; woods outside Shroomville. Each needs a recommendation and a free slot.
  • How to
    1. Salon letters: Lv. 5 plus Marimimi's (or Fulwawa & Fulpapa's, which also pays 10 Diamonds) conversations.

Days 13 – 24

Currency sweep · ends Nov 26, 2:49 PM ET

Events are banked. Now farm what expires and pick up the permanent Diamonds that are close.

  • How to
    1. Path of Practice → Practical Challenge → re-challenge defeated stylist groups with companions. Bring Aricheche, Pachichi, Meyaya, and Tallie or Fussi as you get them.
  • How to
    1. Set off! From Pots to Spira (Tallie), Teenage Shroomling's Rainy Season: Ultimate Headwear (Meyaya), Stepping-Stone Graffiti Challenge (Pachichi), Forced Perspective: Burning Titan Firepit (Jimena). Jimena's repeated conversations pay the “Ever-Glowing Ember” letter.
  • How to
    1. Reunion of Wind and Leaves → Bath letter. Leader Showdown! Child of the Wolf Pack → Path of Practice letter.
  • How to
    1. Bath Lv. 15 = 12k Shroomberry + 40k Stone. Salon Lv. 15 = 19k + 80k. Both drop a Diamond letter.
  • How to
    1. Lv. 10 = 31k Mud + 80k Stone, Radiant Inspiration Set active. Lv. 15 = 55k Mud + 120k Stone and the Dappled Capture headwear.

Not reachable from a fresh start

  • Bath Lv. 30, Drink Shop Lv. 30, Salon Lv. 30, Path of Practice Lv. 25 (Stage III): together about 1.6 million Stone. The whole system makes roughly a third of that in 24 days at max output.
  • Salon Lv. 25 letter (10 Diamonds) and the Lv. 20/25 Resonite milestones: permanent, so they'll still be there in December.
